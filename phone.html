Step 1 of 2

Join the TV

Type the 4-letter code shown on the TV, or scan its QR code with your camera.

Only tiny swing messages go to the TV. Nothing is recorded or saved.

Step 2 of 2

Hold phone like a racket

  1. Grip the bottom of the phone firmly, top pointing up, like a racket handle.
  2. Swing it through: forehand, backhand, or over your head for a smash or serve.
  3. Your player runs to the ball. You just time the swing. 2 players = 2 phones.

Use a wrist strap or case with grip if you have one, and give yourself room to swing.

Your phone may ask for motion access. Only swing messages go to the TV.

Step 2 of 2

Set up the camera

  1. Prop this phone up about 2–3 m away, screen facing you.
  2. 1 or 2 players stand side by side, whole body in the picture, head to feet.
  3. Your arm is the racket: take it back, then swing through.

Your phone will ask to use the camera. Video stays on this phone. Nothing is uploaded or saved.
